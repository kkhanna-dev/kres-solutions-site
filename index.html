FAQ

Questions founders ask first.

Do we actually need SOC 2?

Only if your customers ask for it. That usually starts when you sell to mid-market or enterprise companies. If nobody has asked yet, a Security Assessment and a clear security summary will cover most early deals and save you the audit cost for now.

How long does SOC 2 take?

Getting ready usually takes two to three months. A Type I report can follow soon after. A Type II report needs an observation period, typically three to twelve months, where your controls run and get tested. We help you pick the path that matches your sales timeline.

Can you replace a full-time hire?

For most startups under about 50 people, yes. Our Fractional Partner engagement covers what an early security lead or first sales leader would do, at a fraction of the cost. When you're ready to hire in-house, we help you find and onboard that person.

Do you do the selling for us?

No. We build the process and work alongside you on live deals: qualifying prospects, shaping proposals, pricing, and getting through procurement. Founders stay the face of the company. We make sure they walk into each meeting prepared.

Do you work with our existing tools?

Yes. We work inside your cloud accounts, code repositories, CRM, and compliance platform. If you don't have a tool yet, we recommend one and set it up. We don't resell software.

What does it cost?

It depends on scope, and we'll give you a fixed number after the free assessment. Pricing is by the project or by the month, never by the hour.
