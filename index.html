FAQ

Questions founders ask us first.

Do we actually need SOC 2?

Only if your customers ask for it. That usually starts when you sell to mid-market or enterprise companies. If nobody has asked yet, a Security Sprint and a clear security summary will cover most early deals and save you the audit cost for now.

How long does SOC 2 take?

Getting ready usually takes two to three months. A Type I report can follow soon after. A Type II report needs an observation period, typically three to twelve months, where your controls run and get tested. We help you pick the path that matches your sales timeline.

Can you replace a full-time security hire?

For most startups under about 50 people, yes. Our Fractional Partner engagement covers what an early security hire would do, at a fraction of the cost. When you're ready to hire in-house, we help you find and onboard that person.

Do you work with our existing tools?

Yes. We work inside your cloud accounts, identity provider, code repositories, and compliance platform. If you don't have a tool yet, we recommend one and set it up. We don't resell software.

What does it cost?

It depends on scope, and we'll give you a fixed number after the free assessment. Pricing is by the project or by the month, never by the hour.
