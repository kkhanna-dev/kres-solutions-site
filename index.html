Services

Four things we do well.

Every engagement is scoped to a fixed deliverable with a fixed price. You talk to the engineers doing the work, from the first call through handoff.

Custom software

Internal tools, patient-facing portals, and back-office apps built around your workflow instead of the other way around.

  • Intake, scheduling, and referral tracking tools
  • Reporting dashboards over your existing data
  • Replacing spreadsheet processes with real apps

EHR and data integrations

Get data in and out of Epic and other EHRs without a six-month vendor project. We've shipped FHIR integrations on production clinical platforms.

  • FHIR R4 and HL7 v2 interfaces
  • Epic App Orchard–style app connections
  • Syncs between EHR, billing, and practice tools

Revenue-cycle automation

Fewer denied claims and less rework. We build rules engines and queues that catch coding and eligibility problems before a claim goes out.

  • Pre-submission claim scrubbing and coding checks
  • Denial tracking and appeal workflows
  • 837/835 parsing and reconciliation reports

Security and compliance

Practical security for teams that handle PHI. We'll tell you what actually matters for your size and fix it, rather than hand you a 90-page report.

  • HIPAA readiness reviews and gap fixes
  • Audit logging, access control, and secrets handling
  • Phishing-awareness tooling and staff training

How we work

Small scope, weekly demos, clean handoff.

We'd rather ship a useful first version in four weeks than plan a perfect one for four months.

  1. STEP 1

    Scope

    A 45-minute call about the problem, then a one-page plan: what we'll build, what it costs, and when it lands.

    No charge
  2. STEP 2

    Build

    You see working software every week, not a status slide. Changes in direction get made early while they're cheap.

    Weekly demos
  3. STEP 3

    Harden

    Threat model, access review, audit logging, and backups. This is where most small vendors stop; it's where we spend extra time.

  4. STEP 4
