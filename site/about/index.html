About KRES Solutions

We're a small cybersecurity company in Washington State, started in 2024.

Why we exist

Small and mid-sized businesses use the same Microsoft and cloud tools as big companies, but they rarely have anyone whose job is to keep them secure. Big security firms are too expensive, and most IT providers don't focus on security.

We fill that gap: careful security work, explained clearly, at a price that makes sense for a smaller business.

How we work

We tell you the price before we start. We work in the tools you already have. We explain every change and leave you with notes.

And if you don't need something, we'll tell you.
