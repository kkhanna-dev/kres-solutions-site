About KRES Solutions

KRES Solutions LLC is a Washington security services company founded in December 2024.

Why we started it

Small and mid-sized companies run on the same Microsoft and cloud tools as large enterprises, but they rarely have anyone whose job is to secure them. Large consulting firms are built for the biggest accounts, and many small IT providers don't have real security depth.

We started KRES Solutions to give growing companies careful, security-focused work at a size and price that fits them.

How we work

We are a small firm and focus on a few areas we know well: identity and access, security assessments, and the security of cloud environments and software.

We price clearly, work inside your own tools, and leave you with documentation for everything we set up. If you don't need something, we'll tell you.
