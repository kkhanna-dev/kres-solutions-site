About KRES Solutions

KRES Solutions LLC is a Washington company founded in December 2024 by Kartik Khanna and Rishi Khanna.

Why we started it

We kept seeing the same problem from different sides. Enterprise customers wanted to buy from young software companies but could not get past the security review. Founders had a strong product and no experience running an enterprise sale.

Both problems are solvable with the right preparation, and neither needs a large consulting firm. We started KRES Solutions to do that work for startups directly.

How we operate

We are a small firm and plan to stay that way. We take on a limited number of clients at a time, and the people you meet on the first call are the people who do the work.

We price by the project, work inside your own tools, and leave you with documentation for everything we set up. We would rather tell you that you do not need something yet than sell you work you do not need.
