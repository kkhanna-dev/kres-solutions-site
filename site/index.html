Security and sales support for growing software companies.

KRES Solutions is a consulting firm in the Seattle area. We work with startups that are beginning to sell to larger customers and need help with what comes with it: the customer's security review, and a sales process built for enterprise buyers.

What we do

Security assessments

We review your cloud accounts, code, and access controls, then fix the highest-risk problems. You get a written summary you can share with customers.

SOC 2 and ISO 27001 readiness

We work through the framework with you, write the policies, put the controls in place, and support you through the audit with an independent firm.

Sales and go-to-market

We help you decide who to sell to, set up a pipeline your team will keep up with, and work alongside you on proposals, RFPs, and procurement.

Technology consulting

Architecture reviews, vendor selection, and program management for launches and migrations, from people who have run large technology programs.

More about our services

How an engagement works

Every engagement starts with a free call about where your company is and what is getting in the way. If we can help, we follow up with a short written proposal that sets out the scope, a fixed price, and a timeline.

We do the work inside your own accounts and tools and share progress every week. When we finish, you get documentation for everything we set up, and you keep full ownership of it.

If you don't need something yet, we will tell you.
