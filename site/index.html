What we do

Our practice is built around the Microsoft platform most mid-sized companies already pay for and rarely use to its full potential.

Identity and access

Microsoft Entra ID set up the right way: single sign-on, MFA, Conditional Access, privileged access, and secure access for partners and customers.

Security operations

Microsoft Defender and Sentinel configured, tuned, and monitored, with security assessments and preparation for SOC 2 and ISO 27001.

Azure and cloud infrastructure

Azure environments designed for security and cost from the start, plus migrations, governance, and ongoing operations.

Engineering teams

Experienced engineers and program managers who join your team for a project or on a monthly basis, managed by us.
