Questions

What people usually ask.

We're a small business. Do we really need this?

Small businesses get targeted all the time, mostly through stolen passwords. The basics are quick to fix and cost far less than dealing with a breach.

What do you need from us to start?

A 30-minute call. After that, temporary access to your admin accounts, which we set up with you and remove when we're done.

Which systems do you work with?

Mostly Microsoft 365 and Azure. We also work with Google Workspace, AWS, Google Cloud, and Okta.

How much does it cost?

It depends on the size of your company. After the free call we send a fixed price, so you know the cost before you commit.

Will this disrupt my team?

We plan changes with you and roll them out in stages. Most people only notice a new two-step login prompt.

Not sure how secure you are?

Book a free 30-minute security check. We'll tell you what we see, even if you don't hire us.
