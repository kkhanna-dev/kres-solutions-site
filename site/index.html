What we do

Most security problems at growing companies come down to who can access what. That is where we start.

Identity and access

Single sign-on, MFA, Conditional Access, and admin access cleanup in Microsoft Entra ID, Okta, or Google Workspace, plus sign-in and account security for your own applications.

Security assessments

A review of your Microsoft 365, Azure, or AWS setup against published benchmarks, with the highest-risk issues fixed and a summary you can share with customers.

Cloud and application security

Secure build and deployment pipelines, secrets management, dependency scanning, and audit logging for teams that build their own software.

Compliance preparation

Security questionnaires, policies, and preparation for SOC 2 or ISO 27001 audits, done alongside your team.
