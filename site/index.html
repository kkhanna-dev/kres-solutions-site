What we do

Two things usually slow down a startup's first large deals: the security review and the sales process. We work on both.

Security assessments

We review your cloud accounts, code, and access controls, then fix the highest-risk problems before a customer finds them.

SOC 2 and ISO 27001 readiness

We write the policies, put the controls in place, and support you through the audit with an independent firm.

Sales and go-to-market

We help you pick the right customers, set up a pipeline your team will keep up with, and work alongside you on proposals and procurement.

Technology consulting

Architecture reviews, vendor selection, and program management for launches and migrations.
