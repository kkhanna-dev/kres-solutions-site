Careers

We're building a bench of experienced engineers for client work. Most roles start as contract engagements and can become full-time positions as the firm grows.

What we look for

  • Hands-on experience with Microsoft Entra ID, Intune, Defender, Sentinel, or Azure infrastructure
  • Microsoft certifications such as SC-300, SC-200, AZ-104, or AZ-500 are a plus
  • Clear written communication and comfort working directly with clients
  • Experience working remotely with a small team

How to apply

Email your resume to info@kressolutions.com with "Engineer" in the subject line, and tell us which Microsoft products you know best. We reply when your experience matches upcoming work.

We will never ask candidates for payment, bank details, or government ID numbers by email. If someone claiming to be from KRES Solutions does, please report it to us.
