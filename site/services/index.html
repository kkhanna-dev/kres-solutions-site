Services

We focus on Microsoft security, identity, and cloud. Every engagement has a founder involved and is scoped in writing before work begins.

Identity and access

Microsoft Entra ID

Identity is where most breaches start and where most Microsoft environments have the biggest gaps. We design and implement access controls that protect the company without slowing people down.

  • Single sign-on and MFA rollout across your applications
  • Conditional Access policy design and deployment
  • Privileged Identity Management and admin role cleanup
  • Secure access for partners, vendors, and customers
  • Joiner, mover, and leaver automation with your HR system

Security operations

Microsoft Defender, Sentinel, and Purview

Most companies already own Microsoft security tools that are switched off or left on default settings. We configure them properly, tune the alerts, and make sure someone responds.

  • Security assessments against Microsoft and CIS benchmarks
  • Defender for Endpoint, Office 365, and Cloud Apps configuration
  • Microsoft Sentinel setup, detection rules, and alert tuning
  • Data protection and retention policies with Microsoft Purview
  • Preparation for SOC 2 and ISO 27001 audits

We prepare companies for audits. SOC 2 reports are issued by independent CPA firms and ISO 27001 certificates by accredited certification bodies.

Azure and cloud infrastructure

Microsoft Azure, with AWS and Google Cloud when needed

Cloud environments that are secure, organized, and cost what they should.

  • Azure landing zones, networking, and governance
  • Migrations from on-premises servers or other clouds
  • Device management with Microsoft Intune
  • Cost reviews and right-sizing
  • Backup and disaster recovery planning

Common questions

Do you only work with Microsoft?

Most of our work is in Microsoft environments. We also work with AWS and Google Cloud when clients use them alongside Microsoft 365.

Can you work alongside our IT team?

Yes. Most of our clients have a small IT team. We fill the security and cloud gaps, and we document and train as we go so your team can take over when you're ready.

Where are your engineers located?

Our team is based in the United States and works remotely. We can be on site in the Seattle area when a project calls for it.

How do you use AI?

We use AI-assisted tooling for reviews, documentation, and automation so a smaller team can deliver more. Engineers check all of it, and we never put your data into tools without your approval.
