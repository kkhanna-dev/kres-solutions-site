Services

We focus on the work that sits between a startup and its first large customers. Each engagement is scoped in writing and priced as a fixed fee.

Security assessment

Usually 2 to 3 weeks

A practical review of how your company is set up today, followed by fixes for the problems that matter most. It is a good first step before a customer's security review or a SOC 2 project.

  • Cloud configuration review for AWS, Azure, or Google Cloud
  • Identity and access review, including SSO and multi-factor authentication
  • Review of code repositories, secrets handling, logging, and backups
  • Remediation of the highest-risk findings
  • A written security summary you can share with customers

SOC 2 and ISO 27001 readiness

Usually 8 to 12 weeks before the audit

Preparation for a formal audit, run alongside your team so engineering can keep working on the product.

  • Gap assessment against the framework you need
  • Security policies and procedures written for your company
  • Control implementation and evidence collection
  • Setup of a compliance platform, if you choose to use one
  • Help selecting an audit firm and support during the audit

We do not perform audits. SOC 2 reports are issued by independent CPA firms, and ISO 27001 certificates by accredited certification bodies. We get you ready for them.

Security questionnaires and customer reviews

Per request or ongoing

Large customers often send detailed security questionnaires before they sign. We answer them accurately, keep your answers consistent from one customer to the next, and join calls with customer security teams when needed.

  • Questionnaire responses and a reusable answer library
  • A public security or trust page for your website
  • Support on calls with customer security and procurement teams

Sales and go-to-market

Usually 4 to 6 weeks, with optional ongoing deal support

Selling to enterprises is different from selling to early adopters. We help founders build a process that holds up with larger buyers and work with them on live deals.

  • Target customer profile and positioning
  • Pricing and packaging for larger accounts
  • CRM and pipeline setup
  • Proposal, RFP, and procurement support
  • Cloud marketplace and partner program guidance

Technology consulting

Scoped per project

Advice and hands-on help with technology decisions that are expensive to get wrong.

  • Architecture and IT strategy reviews
  • Vendor and tool selection
  • Program management for launches, migrations, and integrations

Ongoing support

Month to month

For companies that need regular help but are not ready to hire full-time security or sales staff. We agree on the scope each month and cover questionnaires, access reviews, deal support, and anything else from the list above.

Common questions

Do we need SOC 2?

Only if your customers ask for it, which usually starts when you sell to mid-market or enterprise companies. Until then, a security assessment and a clear security summary cover most early deals.

How long does SOC 2 take?

Preparation usually takes two to three months. A Type I report can follow soon after. A Type II report needs an observation period, typically three to twelve months, while your controls operate.

Do you do the selling for us?

No. Founders stay the face of the company. We build the process and work alongside you on qualification, proposals, pricing, and procurement.

How is pricing set?

After the first call we send a proposal with a fixed price for the agreed scope. We do not bill by the hour, and the price only changes if the scope changes in writing.
